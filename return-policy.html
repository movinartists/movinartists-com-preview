Our Commitment

Refund Process Steps

At Movin' Artists, we prioritize your satisfaction. If you are not satisfied with our physical product, please contact us within 7days of your purchase. We will assess your request and work to resolve it swiftly. Refunds may apply based on the evaluation process and the nature of the physical products. Clear communication ensures we can serve you better and maintain our commitment to positive experiences for all our clients. This excludes digital product.

Guidelines for Refunds

To initiate a refund, kindly reach out to our support team via email or phone. You will need to provide your order number and the reason for your request. We aim to review all refund inquiries within five business days. Once approved, you will be notified of the refund status and any further steps involved. We strive to make this process as smooth and transparent as possible.
